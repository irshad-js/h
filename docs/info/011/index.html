Soil-moisture carrier

Hardware ID: 011 · PCB revision: 0.1

Purpose

Analog carrier for a resistive soil-moisture fork.

Power and connection limits

3.3 V accessory port only; analog output stays within the supply range.

Connectors

  • Vinyan IN1–IN4 / JST XH 3-pin: 1=3V3, 2=SOIL, 3=GND.
  • The separate two-pin terminal connects the resistive probe.

Usage and revision notes

  • The probe forms a divider with a 10 kΩ resistor to ground. Wetter soil normally produces a higher reading.
  • Resistive probes corrode under continuous DC excitation; inspect the probe periodically.
