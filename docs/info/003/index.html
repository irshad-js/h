Vaghan

Hardware ID: 003 · PCB revision: 0.1

Purpose

Vaghan RP2354B expansion board with DC motor channels, servo outputs, analog inputs and power conditioning.

Power and connection limits

USB-C PD or barrel input with 3.3 V, 5 V and motor rails. Follow connector labels and revision-specific load limits. Motor/servo loads and startup safety are unqualified on PCB 0.1; do not treat its connector count as an approved current budget.

Connectors

  • J2 is USB-C; J3/J4 are barrel inputs.
  • Motor channels use two dual H-bridge drivers. Servo outputs use J20–J27 JST XH connectors and J28–J33 headers on the 5 V servo rail.
  • Analog inputs expose GPIO40–GPIO47 (ADC0–ADC7). USB, I2C, UART and SWD debug connections are available.

Usage and revision notes

  • The current RP2354B revision is not fabrication-ready. It has outstanding power-path, servo-rail, gate-voltage and motor-startup review items.
  • A future controller redesign is separate hardware and will receive a new permanent ID.
