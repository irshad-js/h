10 mm LED indicator

Hardware ID: 007 · PCB revision: 0.1

Purpose

Single 10 mm LED indicator driven from a Vinyan output.

Power and connection limits

3.3 V accessory port only. The current circuit is intended for a red LED with its fitted series resistor.

Connectors

  • Vinyan OUT1/OUT2 / JST XH 3-pin: 1=3V3, 2=LED, 3=GND.

Usage and revision notes

  • The LED is driven directly through a series resistor. LED colour and resistor substitutions require checking GPIO current and forward voltage.
