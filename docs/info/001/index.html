Vinyan

Hardware ID: 001 · PCB revision: V 1.0

Purpose

Vinyan ESP32 learning board with a CH32 USB/interface controller, discrete LED-matrix drivers, buttons, buzzer and sensor expansion.

Power and connection limits

USB-C or DC barrel input. Confirm the limit printed on your board: this revision's documented DC input limits are under review. Accessory ports and GPIO use 3.3 V only.

Connectors

  • J1 I2C chain, JST XH 4-pin: 1=3V3, 2=GND, 3=SDA, 4=SCL; ESP32 IO4/IO5.
  • JST XH 3-pin accessories: 1=3V3, 2=signal, 3=GND. IN1–IN4 use IO32/33/34/35; OUT1/OUT2 use IO26/27.
  • J11/J14 carry the external LED matrix. J15 exposes ESP32 signals; J12 is the sensor socket.

Usage and revision notes

  • IO34/IO35 are input-only and have no internal pull-up.
  • The LDR and NTC share GPIO39/GPIO36 with J12 through jumpers J17/J18. Remove the appropriate jumper before connecting another sensor on that pin.
  • IO12 on J12 is an ESP32 boot strap: holding it high at reset can prevent boot.
  • This page identifies PCB V 1.0. Existing electrical, thermal and revision review items remain; a QR label does not establish fabrication approval.
