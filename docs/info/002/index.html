Arivaan

Hardware ID: 002 · PCB revision: 1.0

Purpose

Arivaan RP2354B GPIO and logic-analyzer expansion board for the Vinyan ecosystem.

Power and connection limits

USB-C powered with a regulated 3.3 V logic rail. GPIO signals are 3.3 V only. PWRA has no onboard supply; its voltage and current limits require review before external power is connected.

Connectors

  • J1032 is the USB-C input.
  • J1006–J1009 are 1×08 GPIO/display breakout headers. J1016 is a 2×20 socket; J1004/J1005 and J1030/J1031 are 1×16 headers/sockets.
  • J1003 is JST SH 4-pin; J1015 is JST XH 4-pin; J1017–J1019/J1027–J1029 are JST XH 3-pin. Check each connector's printed labels; PWRA is not automatically 3.3 V.
  • S1001/S1002 are tactile buttons; debug access includes SWD/SWCLK.

Usage and revision notes

  • GPIO0 and GPIO1 are connected together on this revision. Do not use default UART0 pins; configure at most one as an output.
  • The input protection and regulator load/thermal budget require qualification. This development revision is not a fabrication approval.
