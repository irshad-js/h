CH224K USB-C PD supply

Hardware ID: 012 · PCB revision: 0.1

Purpose

CH224K USB-C Power Delivery sink supplying a separate DC power input.

Power and connection limits

USB-C PD input; raw VBUS output, normally configured for 12 V or optionally 9 V. Never connect this output to a 3.3 V JST accessory port. Confirm your host board's DC limit before choosing a profile.

Connectors

  • USB-C receptacle is the input.
  • Two-pin output terminal: +=VBUS, −=GND. This connects to an approved host DC/barrel input, not its accessory ports.

Usage and revision notes

  • The configured jumper selects 12 V by default or 9 V. The charger must support the requested profile; a non-PD source supplies 5 V.
  • Vinyan's currently documented DC limits are under review. Do not assume every Vinyan revision accepts the default 12 V profile.
  • This design is intended for 9/12 V profiles, not 15/20 V.
