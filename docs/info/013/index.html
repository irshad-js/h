Single-pin ultrasonic carrier

Hardware ID: 013 · PCB revision: 0.1

Purpose

Single-pin ultrasonic distance-module carrier.

Power and connection limits

3.3 V accessory port only. Use a 3.3 V compatible RCWL-1601 or HC-SR04P; a standard 5 V HC-SR04 is unsuitable.

Connectors

  • Vinyan OUT1/OUT2 / JST XH 3-pin: 1=3V3, 2=SONAR, 3=GND.
  • Module socket order: VCC, TRIG, ECHO, GND.

Usage and revision notes

  • TRIG is driven from SONAR; ECHO connects through 1 kΩ to limit contention.
  • Firmware sends a trigger pulse, switches the pin to input and measures the echo pulse. Use a bidirectional OUT port.
